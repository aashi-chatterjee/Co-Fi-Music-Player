# Co-Fi Music Player 🎶☕️

**Co-Fi** is a simple, handcrafted music player built with **HTML, CSS, and JavaScript**, inspired by the comforting warmth of a cup of coffee. The music selection is thoughtfully curated to enhance moments of study, relaxation, or quiet time in a café.

## 🌟 Features
- **Minimalist design** inspired by a warm cup of coffee
- **Handpicked songs** to elevate your mood and productivity
- **Easy navigation** with previous/next buttons to switch between songs
- **Responsive layout** to enjoy the player on different devices

## 🎧 Perfect for:
- Studying or working
- Relaxing in a café
- Taking a break and unwinding

## 🛠️ Technologies Used
- **HTML** for structure
- **CSS** for styling and design
- **JavaScript** for dynamic song selection and player functionality
